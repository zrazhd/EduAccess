# EduAccess

A simple platform that lets teachers share learning materials with students over a local school network — no internet needed.

---

## What is this?

EduAccess is an open-source web application for schools in areas with limited or no internet access. Teachers upload PDFs, documents, and presentations to a local server. Students connect to the school's Wi-Fi and download whatever they need, right from their phone or laptop.

The idea came from a simple observation: a lot of schools have decent devices but terrible connectivity. Students can't access online resources, and teachers end up printing everything. EduAccess removes both problems — materials live on a local server inside the school and are available to everyone on the network.

---

## The problem it solves

In many regions, schools have computers and smartphones but no reliable internet. Teachers share files over USB drives or messaging apps. There's no structure, no version control, no easy way for students to find what they need.

EduAccess gives schools a lightweight content library that works completely offline. The server runs on any old computer or Raspberry Pi, and students access it through a browser — no app installation required.

---

## Who is it for?

- **Teachers** who want a simple way to share materials with their class
- **Students** who need offline access to course content
- **School IT staff** who need something easy to set up and maintain
- **NGOs and educational programs** working in low-connectivity areas

---

## Features

- Upload materials (PDF, DOCX, PPTX) with subject and class tags
- Browse and filter materials by subject
- One-click download
- Works entirely on a local network — no internet required
- Lightweight — runs on minimal hardware
- Simple admin panel for teachers
